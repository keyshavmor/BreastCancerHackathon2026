For receptor–antibody interactions, the equilibrium dissociation constant is K_D = k_off/k_on. A design with higher model confidence need not have lower K_D. Prediction models largely do not provide calibrated scFv–HER2 dissociation constants at sub-picomolar scale. Treat 0.82 pM as the **contest-provided measured baseline**, not interchangeable with other publications' K_D or EC50 measured under different platforms, immobilization, formats, or assay conditions.

### 1.2 What external trastuzumab evidence supports

- **AlphaBind, 2025:** DMS-informed Trastuzumab-scFv optimization yielded a strongest CDR-H3-only candidate with an independently confirmed **2.8-fold KinExA affinity improvement**, from **7.43 to 2.74 pM**. The wider CDRH2–framework–H3 editing window did not outperform its parent in that particular comparison. This is strong evidence for **focused CDR-H3 engineering**, not proof that the same variants beat the challenge's **0.82 pM BLI** baseline [R6].
- **Mason et al., 2021:** Trastuzumab deep mutational libraries generated substantial labeled HER2-binding data and demonstrated supervised sequence screening [R7]. Such labels are often **binder/nonbinder** or enrichment, not same-platform sub-pM K_D. Avoid conflating them.
- **Qiu et al., 26 October 2026 (AbOptimizer):** In three of five experimentally tested trastuzumab CDR-H3 indel-bearing variants, antibody binding potency improved by an EC50-based assay; the best variant's apparent potency was ~3.2-fold better. **EC50 ≠ K_D**, the tested format was soluble IgG, and indels increase implementation complexity. Use the residue-preservation insight, not the measured figures as promises for our scFv [R8].
- **Framework engineering, 2025:** A trastuzumab case study found that some distal framework mutations can stabilize antibodies while preserving functionality; generic antibody language-model scores may miss framework effects [R9]. Consequently framework preservation/stabilization deserves a small but distinct portfolio branch.
- **PDB 1N8Z:** Experimental structure contains antibody Fab **light chain A**, **heavy chain B**, and **HER2 chain C** [R10]. These must be mapped to the correct variable-domain ranges, excluding constant-domain residues.

**Mutation-numbering warning:** published positions such as W99/G103/F104/Y105 and the WGGDGFYAMD H3 segment are described under differing conventions in various studies. Never transfer residue numbers across IMGT/Kabat/Chothia/PDB/scFv indices without an explicit sequence alignment and a mapping table. For core hotspot residues, confirm identities and coordinates before any freezing or substitution decision.

## 4. ML formulation: constrained robust portfolio optimization

Let s₀ be the exact challenge parent and s a candidate. The (unobserved) true improvement is

    y(s) = log(K_D(s₀) / K_D(s))

which is positive only if measured affinity is stronger (lower K_D). We **cannot reliably evaluate y(s)** without experiment. Instead, use a vector of tractable but imperfect features:

    f(s) = [ΔAbLang2, ΔESM2-PLL, target-specific H3 prior,
            geometry/contact retention, ΔipSAE or Δinterface PAE,
            liability penalty, folding plausibility, sequence novelty].

Use **hard constraints** to ensure the challenge's valid sequence topology, then use conservative Pareto domination, parent-relative evidence, model disagreement and **portfolio diversity**. A scalar score may be produced for triage, but do not conflate its units with experimental affinity; all components require within-model normalization. Prefer rank aggregation over uncalibrated raw sums.

For selecting a set S of 50 from filtered candidates P, a practical heuristic objective is

    maximize_S Σ_{s∈S} quality_rank(s) + λ·coverage(S) − μ·redundancy(S)
    subject to |S|=50, s∈P, branch quotas, and challenge hard constraints.

Where quality_rank is based on Pareto class/robust ranks, `coverage` rewards distinct mutation mechanisms, and `redundancy` penalizes identical or nearly identical mutation signatures. This is **not** calibrated Bayesian optimization. A supervised HER2-affinity predictor is optional only if labeled, compatible data are already locally available and can be tested without leakage; otherwise it is scientifically weaker to add one under time pressure.

### 4.1 Why not train a new model?

The wet-lab signal arrives after the competition, not during the five-hour execution. Training a GP/EVOLVEpro or CNN on binary display labels might be useful if a cleaned dataset and training script already exist, but would transfer imperfectly to this exact scFv format and sub-pM BLI setting. A realistic 5-hour project should prioritize **evidence quality and throughput**, not an unvalidated model that optimizes the wrong target property.

### 4.2 Conservative mutation policy

- Initially use **5–8 substitution mutations** per sequence; lengths remain exactly **242**. This upper limit is self-imposed, not an organizer rule.
- Do not mutate the 15-aa `(GGGGS)₃` linker. Preserve each V_H and V_L domain's correct boundaries.
- Preserve **native cysteines 22, 96, 158, 223** and their disulfides; **forbid new cysteines** in the default run.
- Forbid new N-X-S/T sequons (X ≠ P), screen deamidation, isomerization, unpaired hydrophobics, polybasic patches, extreme charge shifts, and likely buried polar substitutions. **Compare newly introduced** liabilities against parent; do not reject all sequences because the parent contains native motifs.
- Prioritize mutation sites from structure, CDR/IMGT mapping, literature, and AbLang2 proposals. Freeze/highly penalize experimentally established H3 anchors unless targeted evidence justifies a change, with confirmed numbering.
- Beware that “adding harmless framework mutations just to reach five” is *not* automatically safe; multiple substitutions can interact epistatically. Every extra substitution needs a plausible rationale and quality gate.

## 6. Structural preparation, with unambiguous mapping

**Primary reference:** `1N8Z` includes HER2 chain C and antibody Fab chains B (heavy) and A (light) [R10]. Plan:

1. Use the **verbatim organizer CSV sequence supplied above**; write it to `parent_scfv.fasta`. Verify 242 aa, V_H 1–120, linker 121–135, V_L 136–242, cysteines 22/96/158/223, and the SHA-256 hash.

3. Extract **variable portions** of Fab heavy and light chains; align separately to the corresponding parent domains. Do not concatenate entire crystallographic Fab chains (constant domains would corrupt numbering).
4. Generate `residue_map.csv`: `scfv_pos_1based`, `domain`, `domain_pos`, `pdb_chain`, `pdb_resnum`, `pdb_icode`, `parent_aa`, `pdb_aa`, `imgt_number`, `cdr_name`, `min_target_distance_A`, `contact_class`, `resolved_in_pdb`.
5. Compute distance maps between heavy atoms of HER2 and antibody variable domains; identify direct contacts using e.g. **4.5–5.0 Å**, near contacts **5–8 Å**, and buried core separately. These are *exploratory geometry bins*, not learned affinity thresholds.
6. Extract **HER2 domain IV only** from the PDB/UniProt mapping, including the experimentally observed epitope and enough domain-IV structural context to avoid cutting the fold. **Do not cofold the full HER2 ectodomain**. 7. Compare the parent modeled scFv–HER2 to the experimental complex: epitope-contact recovery, orientation, framework stability, steric clashes, and interface-specific confidence. Do not rank candidate scores before the parent passes a minimal geometry sanity check.

Use the **same domain-IV crop for parent and every candidate**, verify the reference epitope and parental geometry, and record these limitations. If the parent fails geometry QC, note scores as low-confidence; do **not** fall back to prohibited full-ectodomain cofolding.

## 7. Computational generation and ranking stages

### Stage G: structure- and evidence-guided proposal pool

**Preferred size:** 10,000–30,000 sampled combinatorial proposals; filtering is CPU-heavy and relatively cheap **after** the per-site AbLang2 masked scan. Restrict masked scanning to a verified list of plausible mutable sites and retain only a handful of alternatives per site. Mutate fixed positions, preserve high-value anchor sites and the scFv topology.

Construct the four branches independently, not by randomly labeling one homogeneous pool. Each proposal records origin, per-site support, mutation count, parent distance, liabilities, CDR categories, motif lineage, random seed, and all computational model versions.

Avoid unnecessary 20,000-design contextual AbLang2 rescoring and AbRep embeddings. Reserve expensive scoring for ~300–600 diverse proposals if time permits. If AbLang2 cannot load/validate, use only **explicitly labeled** conservative BLOSUM-like/structural candidate generation as a fallback; do not falsely describe it as an AbLang2 model run.

### Stage E: ESM2-3B evidence, not experimental affinity

Score a preselected diverse subset (initial cap **150–300**) with the installed `esm2_t36_3B_UR50D` backend. First benchmark throughput on 10 sequences; then set a hard stop based on available time. Cheap full-sequence log probabilities are preferred first. For a much smaller set (e.g. 40–100 candidates), optionally perform context-masked per-mutated-residue pseudo-log-likelihood and **parent-relative substitutions at identical positions**, in identical precision and preprocessing conditions.

Model memory: 3B parameters alone require roughly 6 GB in FP16/BF16 or 12 GB in FP32, **excluding** activations, PyTorch buffers and caching. A 24 GB GPU may manage one model, but do not overlap a large ESM model and Boltz-2. Prefer mixed precision if model/hardware support it; verify a small subset for numeric stability and no NaN/Inf; do not silently change the foundation model to fit memory.

Select **50 provisional** candidates at this stage, then increase to ~60–80 if there is time and additional structural scoring capacity. Persist a valid `final_50_provisional.fasta` before allocating the structural budget.

### Stage B: Boltz-2 matched baseline and bounded validation

First run the **parent against HER2 domain IV** and measure cold-start latency, peak VRAM, preprocessing/MSA time, GPU inference and completeness of structure/PAE/ipSAE output. Never run a full 630-residue HER2 cofold during this challenge, even for the parent. Never transfer throughput estimates from another complex size.

**Target budget:** **parent + 50 candidate cofolds if the microbenchmark proves this feasible**, with **15 distinct scored candidates as the first priority** and 50–100 *sequence candidates* reserved for later cofolding when resources permit. Do not cofold before sequence screening. Run with a consistent domain-IV crop, MSA/preprocessing mode, seed/sampling policy, recycling/precision and metric settings. After parent and first 3 candidates, estimate how many additional complete predictions (including ipSAE postprocessing) fit into the remaining wall-clock. If 50 will not fit, prioritize a diversity-aware 15; never fill remaining ipSAE cells with estimates.

Compute **actual external ipSAE for each candidate intended for submission** from the matching full PAE + structure output, using the verified DunbrackLab/IPSAE implementation (R16), preserving version, numerical cutoffs, score aggregation, chain mapping and residue mask. Do not substitute protein–ligand Boltz-2 affinity predictions: **Boltz-2's small-molecule affinity head is not a calibrated antibody–HER2 K_D estimator** [R12]. Normalize structural metrics relative to parent. Also inspect contact recovery and new interface clashes/unsatisfied polar groups when practical.

**MSA:** Reuse target MSA across variants only if the installed software and file format permit. Existing repository warns `--use_msa_server` can rate-limit and idle the GPU; plan to cache or precompute. An empty-MSA fallback is allowed only as a **separately documented, matched parent-and-candidate protocol** (potentially lower confidence). Do not merge rankings from different MSA conditions uncritically.

### Stage Q: multi-objective, diversity-aware portfolio selection

Mandatory first-pass filters: compliant format/sequence, no new cysteines, no linker changes, clear domain mapping, no newly introduced severe liabilities, deduplication, mutation-count check. Second-pass: veto gross interface loss, severe model confidence deterioration, poor structural geometry, extreme ESM2 penalties, or very close duplication. Remaining candidates: assign ordinal ranks or percentile-normalized features *within the same pipeline conditions*.

Example auditable **triage** score, not an affinity equation:

    rank_score = 0.25 * H3_evidence_rank
               + 0.20 * AbLang2_rank
               + 0.15 * ESM2_delta_rank
               + 0.20 * geometry_parent_retention_rank
               + 0.10 * structural_confidence_rank
               + 0.10 * developability_rank

This is a starting hypothesis, not a validated set of weights. If a candidate lacks Boltz results, mark its structural confidence as `NOT_EVALUATED` and **exclude that sequence from the ipSAE-complete organizer submission**. Keep it in the clearly separate internal 50-row proposal table. Handle screened/unscreened candidates in explicit evidence tiers. Do **not** use branch labels as affinity evidence, and do not select pure geometric winners just because high ipSAE is a judging criterion. For 50, enforce branch-diversity quotas, minimum mutation-set distance where feasible, and limited near-duplicates. For the top 15, require detailed per-candidate justifications and uncertainty flags.

### Mandatory ipSAE acceptance gate (not an optional enhancement)

For each scored submission row, require a chain-resolved complex output, full 2-D PAE matrix, and successful external ipSAE calculation. Save `boltz_run_dir`, `structure_path`, `pae_path`, `ipsae_stdout_path`, `ipsae_script_version`, `pae_cutoff_A`, `distance_cutoff_A`, `ipsae_min`, `ipsae_max_or_directional_if_reported`, `model_seed`, `target_domain_iv_definition`, and `status=PASSED`. Each calculated ipSAE must be numeric/finite; reject a candidate if the metric output is missing, if chain indices are wrong, or if PAE/structure residue counts mismatch. Compare a parent baseline under exactly the same configuration and retain raw logs.

**Important:** Boltz outputs are not affinity estimates. A difference of 0.01 in ipSAE is not a demonstrated K_D gain. The final 15 should balance robust interface preservation, plausible antibody sequence changes, expression risk and mutation diversity; use ipSAE primarily to identify incorrect/broken models and to meet the scoring requirement. If a method requires scores for all 50, budget the number of final sequence proposals dynamically to the number of Boltz runs that can actually complete, rather than submitting 50 unscored sequences.

### 8.1 Runtime budgeting formula and logging

After timing `t_parent` and `t_variant` wall-clock durations (including preprocessing and inference), schedule at most

    N_fit = max(0, floor((remaining_structure_minutes - safety_buffer_minutes) / t_variant))

with a dynamic cap (target 50, minimum-priority 15). Re-estimate after each small batch. Log GPU `memory.used`, `memory.total`, GPU util, CPU RAM, sample length, PAE output size, model/weights hash, per-seed time, and any MSA waiting. A single 24 GB device should run **one major GPU model at a time**. Delete released model objects and verify VRAM is available before switching from ESM2 to Boltz-2.

Pre-download all required weights **before the contest clock if rules allow**. Also prepare a single conda environment or verified environments, ensure disk capacity for large model caches, and smoke test exact CLI flags. If pre-staging is prohibited, reflect download latency in the 300-minute budget.

### 8.2 Adaptive fallback matrix

| Trigger | Action | How to report |
|---|---|---|

| AbLang2 unavailable | Conservative structure-based/BLOSUM proposals, *not mislabeled* as model-generated | `GENERATOR_FALLBACK` |
| ESM2 OOM | Reduce batch size first; use verified half precision; reduce sample count | Preserve model identity and numeric checks |
| ESM2 too slow | Stop after bounded subset; rerank with available AbLang2 data | `ESM_NOT_EVALUATED` on others |

| Fewer than 15 genuine ipSAE outputs | Export only measured score-complete subset; urgently request organizer help if time permits | Never submit null or modeled ipSAE as measured |

## 9. Scientific evaluation, sensitivity and no-leakage policy

**Minimum analyses:**
- Parent vs every candidate: mutation count and site list, domain/CDR mapping, AbLang2/ESM2 delta, liability changes, structural score difference **where measured**, known-contact retention, and uncertainty.
- At least one alternative ranking or robustness check: rank aggregation rather than arbitrary raw-score sums; re-select top 15 with altered weights; report which candidates are stable and which depend on one metric.
- Exposure to reference data: if using published DMS, record source and labeling method (binding classification, enrichment, apparent affinity, actual K_D). No disguised single-label conversion to pM units.
- Avoid overfitting predicted geometry: compare to known experimental epitope, inspect failure modes, choose some candidates with distinct mutation hypotheses even if slightly lower proxy scores.
- Report the 15 candidates' expected risk and evidence rather than asserting any predicted fold improvement in K_D.

**Recommended primary scientific claim:** “We combined literature-informed, antibody-specific mutation priors with general PLM plausibility, experimentally grounded interface preservation, parent-relative complex confidence, and constrained portfolio optimization.”

**Claims to prohibit:** “Boltz predicts 0.1 pM K_D”; “ipSAE improvement proves higher affinity”; “all 50 are experimentally validated”; “Anthropic's de novo H100 throughput generalizes to a 24 GB RTX”; “dataset binary binding labels equal pM affinity.”

## 12. Open questions and falsifiable assumptions for Claude to challenge

1. Does the exact 242-aa organizer parent align residue-for-residue with the variable domains of `1N8Z`? If not, which positions differ, and what is the coordinate mapping confidence?
2. Are published trastuzumab CDR-H3 improved variants within five to eight substitutions of this exact parent? Do they involve indels or different antibody format? Can any be used as *sequence priors* without accidental violation of contest restrictions?
3. Which **exact domain-IV residue boundaries** and target-chain sequence are justified by 1N8Z/P04626? Does the domain-IV parent cofold reproduce experimental epitope and orientation? Full-ectodomain cofolding is out of scope.
4. Does ESM2-3B/AbLang2 rank known trastuzumab binders above known nonbinders on a small matched, *non-overlapping* test set? Does adding sequence evidence improve robustness relative to naive BLOSUM proposals?
5. Does Boltz produce reproducible, physically plausible parent geometry? If it cannot, are ipSAE deltas valid evidence at all?
6. Does the 0.82 pM challenge parent use a specific laboratory scFv preparation, orientation and kinetic fit that differs from literature baselines? How should potential assay-floor and rebinding effects be discussed?
7. Are we permitted to preinstall models, download datasets or run preprocessing before the challenge clock starts? If not, budgets must be tightened.
8. Does `proteinbase.com/protein-design-skills` contain procedures relevant to **local mutation optimization** rather than de novo binder generation? Which installed Claude skills are actually helpful, and which add overhead?
9. What exact file schema does the organizer require for sequence + ipSAE + explanation? The new guidance permits multiple proposed designs and demands ipSAE for **each submitted sequence**, while the earlier slide limits lab testing to 15/team. Submit only real score-complete records.

## 13. Final acceptance criteria

The challenge run is **successful as a computational workflow** if (i) 50 valid unique *internal* candidate sequences exist, (ii) a scored submission subset has actual ipSAE **for every included design** and ideally includes 15 ranked nominees, (iii) domain-IV mapping and compliance are independently verified, (iv) every reported model score has genuine stored outputs and conditions, and (v) the team explains the method and uncertainty. **No experimental improvement claim** is justified until the assay returns K_D/k_on/k_off and sensorgrams.
